Build your shortcut — pick what it should do, then add it.

What your shortcut will open
  1. 1
    Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to create a new shortcut.
  2. 2
    Tap the search bar, find URL Encode, and add it. Set its input to Shortcut Input.
  3. 3
    Add Open URLs underneath it.
  4. 4
    In the Open URLs box, paste the address below (use Copy link above), then tap right after it and insert the URL Encoded Text variable at the very end:
  5. 5
    Tap the ⓘ button at the bottom and turn on Show in Share Sheet.
  6. 6
    Name it Ashgrab, choose the icon you like, and tap Done.

Your iPhone will ask you to confirm before adding a shortcut it didn't download from Apple — that's normal. If it refuses, open Settings → Shortcuts and turn on Allow Untrusted Shortcuts (the switch only appears after you've run one shortcut at least once).
